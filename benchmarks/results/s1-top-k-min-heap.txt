Bifrost — S1: Top-K Min-Heap

Optimization:
Replace full result sorting with a size-K min-heap.

Goal:
Avoid sorting every scored candidate when only the top K results
are requested.

Environment:
- CPU: Apple M2
- OS: macOS
- Architecture: arm64
- Go benchmark package: internal/search
- Benchmark: BenchmarkSearch
- Benchmark flags: -benchmem -count=3
- K: 10

------------------------------------------------------------
4. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Added resultHeap using Go's container/heap package.
- Maintains at most K results during ranking.
- The heap root represents the worst currently selected result.
- A better result replaces the root.
- Final sorting is performed only on the retained results.
- Preserves ranking order:
    1. Higher score first.
    2. Lexicographically smaller DocID first for equal scores.

Scoring and candidate generation remain unchanged.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

S1 reduces ranking work from full sorting, O(N log N), to
Top-K heap selection, O(N log K), followed by sorting K results.

The 100,000-document synthetic benchmark showed approximately
33.9% lower search latency.

The heap optimization is retained.

Benchmark results are workload-dependent and should be remeasured
under controlled conditions before making definitive performance
claims.
